## Universal metadata rules

- `LAND`, `YARD`, and `COMP` pieces use bottom-center pivots based on structural ground contact.
- `FENCE-01–30` and `ARCH-25–28` require deterministic 32-pixel seam normalization after extraction.
- Large trees receive trunk-only collision and may need split canopy/trunk layers.
- Flowers, grass, low ground cover, and most planters remain nonblocking.
- Gates, fences, walls, benches, carts, storage, and architectural additions receive object-specific collision only where structurally justified.
- Any composite that cannot support correct player overlap must be separated into ground, structural, and foreground layers or rejected.
